How the radar works

Entries are maintained from hobby press — Toyark, Marvel Toy News, and Bleeding Cool — and from Hasbro Pulse announcements. Prices are announced MSRP at reveal, not market prices. A figure graduates from the radar into the timeline archive once it's released and documented. Statuses change fast; the source link is the authority, not this page.
