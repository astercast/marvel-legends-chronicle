The line, called by name

The Roll Call.

Seven hundred distinct characters stand on this roll — and the line does not treat them evenly. A handful anchor it, returning figure after figure across decades of waves; most are called exactly once and never again. This census counts every character by the figures the catalog gives them: the head of the roll, the bands they fall in, and the long tail at its true size. A character is counted by the archive's own rule, the name with any parenthetical removed, and the roll is ordered by count, then by earliest year, then by catalog order — a fact of the record, never an editorial ranking.

Counted in this browser from the catalog itself (1,939 figures). A character is its name with any parenthetical removed — the debut ledger's rule, shared so the pages cannot disagree; nothing else is normalized, so names the catalog files separately are counted separately. A character's count is its catalog figures; its span is its first and last figure years; its wave count is the distinct waves its figures appear in. The bands are fixed ranges of the count, and every band sums back to the archive's own totals. This desk keeps no prices: a census of the roll is the record, not the market. For each character's first figure, read First Appearances; for the silences between figures, read The Long Wait.
