Don't get burned — the checklist

Read this before you pay anything over retail, or buy anything on a marketplace. Every line comes from the authentication wizard or the 2026 market briefing.

Figure-specific KO risk: Maximum Spider-Man and X-Men '97 Cyclops

A Maximum Series Spider-Man KO was reviewed in video around April 2026, and an X-Men '97 Cyclops KO (Mustang Toyz, S.H. Figuarts-style) in September 2026. Buy these two at retail or Hasbro Pulse — treat marketplace copies as guilty-until-proven-innocent and run them through the authentication wizard.

The classic telltales still work

Packaging: blurry printing, off-color logos, missing Hasbro/Marvel marks, or packaging that doesn't match the figure's era. Paint: slop, rough edges, missing applications. Joints: peg shapes that differ from reference photos, unexplained gaps at elbows/knees — plus the documented Carnage tell: silver eyes on the KO. Community consensus also flags brittle joints (one reported Black Cat KO leg snap).

Listing + price logic

Stock photos only, or "no box" on a supposedly mint exclusive — walk away. Seller in China/Hong Kong with $10–40 pricing on a $25+ figure — walk away. And the market is the lie detector: 40%+ under 90-day eBay solds = assume bootleg until proven otherwise. Asking prices are wishes; only solds count.

The modern chase is dead — don't pay chase premiums

Hasbro has "largely abandoned" short-packed chase figures; no 2025–2026-era 6" chase was documented by the hobby press this year. If a seller asks a "chase" premium on 2026 product, that's a markup looking for a story — the price guide shows how to check what's real.

Reissue timing: the premium-killer

The pattern is textbook: sellout → aftermarket spike → reissue → premium collapse (the TAS "VHS" exclusives, reissued on retro cards). Before paying over retail, check the pre-order tracker and radar for an announced reissue — vulnerable premiums rest on availability and can collapse overnight.

How this guide stays honest

All picks are researched releases from data/preorders.json (observation date October 11, 2026; statuses frozen there). "MSRP" means the price announced at reveal, per the wave-12 hobby-press research — not a market price. Where no price was reported, the card says so and links pre-filled eBay sold listings (actual sales, not asking prices) so you can check.
