Build-A-Figure math

BAF economics.

What does it actually cost to finish each Build-A-Figure? Every dollar below comes from sold listings you logged in your journal, no market index, no scraped prices, no guesses.

No journal data yet

BAF economics runs on your sold-sighting log. Head to the price journal, paste a few eBay sold listings for figures whose BAF pieces you need, and this page will rank every Build-A-Figure by what it costs you to finish.

Open the journal

Method: for each BAF piece figure, we take the median of your journal sold entries matched to that figure (same matching as the journal's tracked value). Completion cost is the sum of piece medians. Pieces with no logged sales are flagged "no data" and contribute $0, a BAF with unpriced pieces is shown as a partial estimate, never a full price. Owned pieces are subtracted when the toggle is on. This is your log, not a market index.
