From the registrar's desk

Why this archive catalogues the chase variants

Twenty-four years of this line, and here's what I've learned keeping its records: the official checklists always forget the interesting parts. They remember the wave. They forget the running change that swapped a head sculpt mid-production, the retailer one-off that only existed for a single Christmas, the chase variant packed one-per-case that nobody admitted to until the auction listings proved it.

So this archive catalogues those too. Every entry is researched — sourced, dated, and honest about what's unconfirmed. Pre-orders live in the tracker, not the archive: we don't catalogue figures that don't exist yet. And nothing here is invented. When a listing says TBA, it's because the truth is TBA, not because the research stopped.

The Toy Biz years are my soft spot — clamshells, a 32-page comic in every box, and joints that creaked like a screen door. Hasbro made the line smoother. Whether they made it better is a bar fight I'll happily lose. Pull a drawer and decide for yourself.
