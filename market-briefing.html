Gift-season market notes

Standard green Epic Heroes Doom is not the mover: Legendsverse tracks 48 sales with an aftermarket average of $62.44 (checked Oct 2026); recent Jul–Aug 2026 solds landed $40–$75. It's the white variant that carries the premium, not the character.

Stock snapshot (BigBadToyStore, Oct 2026): the older Cyclops (Colossus BAF) sat in stock at $14.99 (40% off) while X-Men '97 Cyclops was sold out and the Cyclops/Jean two-pack sat on waitlist. That's a snapshot of one retailer's stock, not a trend — but it shows where the 2026 demand concentrates.

The reissue program is accelerating, not cooling: Retro Venom — the 2021 Pulse Con cel-shaded exclusive — is back at Fan Channel for $34.99, shipping December 2026 (Bleeding Cool); the TAS retro wave ($27.99 each) now has a reported ship window of December 2026/January 2027 (ToyHypeUSA); and NYCC added an Astonishing X-Men Cyclops & Wolverine 2-pack reissue, pre-order October 28 (single source: Mephitsu). Every one of these is a reissue-radar vulnerable candidate resolving live.

Knockoffs — no new 2026 wave documented: the holiday KO watch stays what it was — Maximum Series Spider-Man KO (video-reviewed) and the X-Men '97 Cyclops KO, with the Carnage silver-eyes telltale as the reference case. Chase figures

The modern chase is effectively dead

Hasbro has "largely abandoned" short-packed chase figures in modern Marvel Legends. No 2025–2026-era 6" chase figure was documented by the hobby press this year. For the full chase/variant/exclusive/limited taxonomy — documented examples, spotting tells, and what rarity means for value — see the Rarity Guide.

The modern chase-adjacent standout is the Epic Heroes (3.75") white Dr. Doom — the "Future Foundation" variant — with reported eBay solds of $115–$200 (May–Aug 2026) against roughly $60 in October 2024, per actionfigure411's sold-history page. Holiday-season check (Oct 11, 2026): the most recent reported sold on that tracker was $200.00 on Aug 10, 2026 — sitting at the top of the band (Mar 12: $150; May 3: $149). Treat that as reported solds on one tracker, not a valuation.

Grail of the season

The $7,500 Wasp prototype

An unproduced Toy Biz Marvel Legends Series 15 blue-and-white Wasp chase prototype — one of only ~10 carded examples known since 2006 — was listed on eBay at $7,500, reported as the single most sought-after ML figure. That is an asking price, not a completed sale, and the prototype is not a 2026 discovery — it is an active listing for a two-decade-old artifact.

Reissue pressure

Hasbro's reissue program is cooling aftermarket premiums

The Pulse "VHS"-exclusive Spider-Man: The Animated Series (and X-Men) figures escalated on the aftermarket after their sellouts. Hasbro's 2026 answer: re-release those same figures on retro blister cards (the TAS wave — Aunt May, Mary Jane, Smythe, Carnage — pre-ordered September 29, 2026), directly softening the availability-based premiums the VHS versions built up. The pattern is textbook: sellout → aftermarket spike → reissue → premium collapse.

The same vulnerability framing lives in the encyclopedia hall of fame: resilient premiums rest on true rarity, vulnerable ones rest on availability and can collapse when Hasbro reissues.

Knockoffs

The KO market is evergreen, not a 2026 wave

No notable 2025–2026-specific counterfeit wave was documented. What exists is the steady AliExpress KO trade targeting current hits:

  • Maximum Series Spider-Man KO — reviewed in video, ~April 2026.
  • X-Men '97 Cyclops KO (Mustang Toyz, S.H. Figuarts-style) — reviewed September 2026.
  • Older Spider-Man KOs (Pizza Spidey, Gamerverse, AND, 2099) and Carnage KOs with known telltales: silver eyes on Carnage; neck-ball/elbow peg differences and joint gaps on Spider-Man.

Community consensus (Toyark forums): buyer beware — inconsistent paint and brittle joints (one reported Black Cat KO leg snap). If a listing looks wrong, run it through the authentication wizard and the bootleg-spotting guide.
