# Decide whether to grade a figure

## Use when

Before sending any carded figure to AFA/CGA, CAS, or UKG.

## Inputs

- The figure
- Its honest card grade (C-scale)
- Its ungraded market value

## Procedure

1. Grade the card honestly first (see the card-grading skill). C10 is a myth; most 'mint' is C8. Only C8.5+ candidates are worth the conversation.
2. Know the vendors: AFA inside CGA (Roswell GA; NY drop-off since Sept 2026; 0–100 in 5s; C/B/F sub-grades); CAS (independent, anti-U-grade, BBTS partner since Nov 2025); UKG (UK-based). TGA is CLOSED — do not send there.
3. Historic costs only (verify current before sending): ~$16 standard / $28 express (old), ~$45+ with shipping (2008), up to ~$90–100 all-in for large items (2012); CAS ~$100 all-in (2023–24 anecdote). Turnarounds historically 30–90+ days.
4. The no-premium caveat: NO Legends-specific graded-vs-ungraded sale premium has been documented. Graded SW anecdotes run 1/3–2× ungraded AND below purchase price — both directions, both anecdotes.
5. Decision rule: grade for authentication, preservation, or buyer confidence on a figure worth 5×+ the grading cost. Never grade a $30 figure expecting a markup — the math doesn't work.

## Worked example

$45 figure, C9 card, $60 grading cost: don't grade. $400 grail, C8.5, authentication doubt: grade for the buyer confidence.
