# Collect the Transition years (2007–2009)

## Use when

Understanding 2007–2009 releases and their quirks.

## Inputs

- Knowledge of the license handoff

## Procedure

1. Learn the handoff story, it explains packaging and distribution quirks.
2. Watch for short-packed waves; some transition figures are sleeper grails.
3. Cross-reference waves by year in the Chronicle timeline.

## Worked example

Transition-era short-packs often out-appreciate their Hasbro successors.

- `data/encyclopedia.json`
- `data/figures.json`

## Pitfalls

- Assuming Hasbro-era pricing logic applies, transition supply was chaotic.
