# Collect the Toy Biz era (2002–2006)

## Use when

Collecting or evaluating 2002–2006 Toy Biz releases.

## Inputs

- Era knowledge: 6-inch scale launch, BAF innovation, legendary sculpts

## Procedure

1. Start with the Hall of Fame Toy Biz entries, they define the era's value.
2. Authenticate everything: bootleg density is highest here.
3. Condition is king, card condition drives Toy Biz premiums.
4. Key lines: Series 1–9, Face-Off, X-Men Classics, Marvel Select crossovers.

## Worked example

A Series 1 Toad: verify the initial-assortment markers before paying grail money.

## Pitfalls

- Loose Toy Biz at boxed prices, loose is common, boxed is the premium.
