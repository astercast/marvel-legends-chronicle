# Understand the HasLab era (2020+)

## Use when

Evaluating HasLab projects and their aftermarket.

## Inputs

- The HasLab project list
- Backer counts

## Procedure

1. HasLab = limited by backers, not by retail, scarcity is real.
2. Aftermarket peaks 6–12 months post-delivery, then settles.
3. Never back what you can't afford to keep, flipping HasLab is a gamble.

## Worked example

Galactus: backer-count scarcity drove a 2× aftermarket before settling.

## Pitfalls

- Backing for flip profit, the settle hurts.
