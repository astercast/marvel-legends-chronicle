# Complete the Nemesis (Uncanny X-Force) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Nemesis (Uncanny X-Force) BAF from the 2025 Nemesis Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Nemesis (Uncanny X-Force)** (2025, Nemesis Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Cyclops → Right arm
  - [ ] Fabian Cortez → Left leg
  - [ ] Gambit → Right leg
  - [ ] Husk → Torso and skull
  - [ ] Marrow → Left arm
  - [ ] Nightcrawler → Helmet
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Nemesis (Uncanny X-Force) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
