# Complete the Mandroid Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Mandroid BAF from the 2014 Mandroid Wave (Infinite Series) wave. 5 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Mandroid** (2014, Mandroid Wave (Infinite Series)).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Baron Helmut Zemo → Right arm
  - [ ] Black Widow → Torso
  - [ ] Captain America → Head, Right leg
  - [ ] Red Skull → Left arm
  - [ ] Winter Soldier → Left leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Mandroid torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Data references

- `data/figures.json`

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
