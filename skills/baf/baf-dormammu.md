# Complete the Dormammu Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Dormammu BAF from the 2016 Dormammu Wave wave. 7 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Dormammu** (2016, Dormammu Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Astral Doctor Strange → Head
  - [ ] Brother Voodoo → Left arm
  - [ ] Doctor Strange → Left leg, Right arm
  - [ ] Enchantress → Upper torso
  - [ ] Iron Fist → Shoulder pads and chest cap
  - [ ] Karl Mordo → Right leg and flaming skulls
  - [ ] Nico Minoru → Lower torso
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Dormammu torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
