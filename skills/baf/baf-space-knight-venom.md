# Complete the Space Knight Venom Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Space Knight Venom BAF from the 2016 Space Knight Venom Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Space Knight Venom** (2016, Space Knight Venom Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Electro → Right leg
  - [ ] Hobgoblin → Head
  - [ ] Silk → Left leg
  - [ ] Spider-Girl (Ashley Barton) → Torso
  - [ ] Spider-Man (Miles Morales) → Right arm
  - [ ] Ultimate Spider-Man → Left arm
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Space Knight Venom torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
