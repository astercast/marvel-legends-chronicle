# Complete the Kree Sentry Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Kree Sentry BAF from the 2019 Kree Sentry Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Kree Sentry** (2019, Kree Sentry Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Captain Marvel (Genis-Vell) → Right leg
  - [ ] Carol Danvers and Goose → Left arm
  - [ ] Grey Gargoyle → Head
  - [ ] Nick Fury and Goose → Right arm
  - [ ] Talos → Left leg
  - [ ] Yon-Rogg → Torso
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Kree Sentry torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
