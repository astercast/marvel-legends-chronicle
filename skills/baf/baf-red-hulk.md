# Complete the Red Hulk Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Red Hulk BAF from the 2008 Red Hulk Wave (Target Exclusive) wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Red Hulk** (2008, Red Hulk Wave (Target Exclusive)).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Adam Warlock → Left leg
  - [ ] Silver Surfer → Right leg
  - [ ] Spider-Man → Left arm
  - [ ] Spiral → Head
  - [ ] Union Jack → Torso
  - [ ] Wolverine → Right arm
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Red Hulk torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
