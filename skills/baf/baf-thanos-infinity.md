# Complete the Thanos (Infinity) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Thanos (Infinity) BAF from the 2015 Thanos Wave wave. 5 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Thanos (Infinity)** (2015, Thanos Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Batroc → Torso
  - [ ] Captain America → Right leg
  - [ ] Hellcat → Left arm and head
  - [ ] Hulk → Right arm
  - [ ] Iron Man → Left leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Thanos (Infinity) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
