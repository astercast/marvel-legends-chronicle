## Use when

You want to build the Holocaust (Nemesis) BAF from the 2009 Nemesis Wave (Walmart Exclusive) wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Holocaust (Nemesis)** (2009, Nemesis Wave (Walmart Exclusive)).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Beast → Right arm
  - [ ] Black Bolt → Right leg
  - [ ] Daredevil → Lower torso
  - [ ] Nova (Richard Rider) → Left leg
  - [ ] Punisher → Left arm

3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.
