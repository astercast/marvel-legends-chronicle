# Complete the Gilgamesh (Eternals) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Gilgamesh (Eternals) BAF from the 2021 Gilgamesh Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Gilgamesh (Eternals)** (2021, Gilgamesh Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Druig → Head
  - [ ] Kingo → Right arm and alternate hand
  - [ ] Makkari → Left arm and alternate hand
  - [ ] Phastos → Left leg
  - [ ] Sersi → Right leg
  - [ ] Sprite → Torso
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Gilgamesh (Eternals) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
