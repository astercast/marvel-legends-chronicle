# Complete the Arnim Zola Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Arnim Zola BAF from the 2012 Arnim Zola Wave (Return of Marvel Legends) wave. 8 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Arnim Zola** (2012, Arnim Zola Wave (Return of Marvel Legends)).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Captain America (Bucky Barnes) → Right leg
  - [ ] Dark Wolverine (Daken) → Left arm
  - [ ] Drax the Destroyer → Left leg
  - [ ] Fantomex → Right arm
  - [ ] Madame Hydra (Viper) → Torso
  - [ ] Madame Masque → Torso
  - [ ] Piledriver → Head (red eye) and control
  - [ ] Thunderball → Head (yellow eye) and control
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Arnim Zola torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
