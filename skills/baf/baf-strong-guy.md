# Complete the Strong Guy Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Strong Guy BAF from the 2020 Strong Guy Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Strong Guy** (2020, Strong Guy Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Black Tom Cassidy → Back
  - [ ] Deadpool → Head, Left leg
  - [ ] Maverick → Left arm
  - [ ] Shiklah and Jeff the Baby Land Shark → Right arm
  - [ ] Sunspot → Torso
  - [ ] Warpath → Right leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Strong Guy torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

- `data/figures.json`

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
