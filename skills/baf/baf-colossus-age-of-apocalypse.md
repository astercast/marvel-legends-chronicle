# Complete the Colossus (Age of Apocalypse) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Colossus (Age of Apocalypse) BAF from the 2021 Colossus Wave (Age of Apocalypse) wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Colossus (Age of Apocalypse)** (2021, Colossus Wave (Age of Apocalypse)).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Cyclops → Left leg
  - [ ] Iceman → Right arm and alternate hand
  - [ ] Legion → Right leg
  - [ ] Rogue → Left arm and alternate hand
  - [ ] Sabretooth → Head
  - [ ] Shadowcat → Torso
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Colossus (Age of Apocalypse) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
