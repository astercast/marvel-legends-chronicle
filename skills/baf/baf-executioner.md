# Complete the Executioner Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Executioner BAF from the 2025 Executioner Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Executioner** (2025, Executioner Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Enchantress → Torso
  - [ ] Iron Man → Head and axe
  - [ ] Phantom Rider → Left leg
  - [ ] Spider-Man (Ai Apaec) → Right leg
  - [ ] Warbow → Right arm
  - [ ] Werewolf by Night → Left arm and alternate hand
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Executioner torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
