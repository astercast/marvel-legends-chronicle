# Complete the The Watcher (What If...?) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the The Watcher (What If...?) BAF from the 2021 The Watcher Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **The Watcher (What If...?)** (2021, The Watcher Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Captain Carter → Arms
  - [ ] Doctor Strange Supreme → Left leg
  - [ ] Heist Nebula → Torso
  - [ ] Star-Lord (T'Challa) → Head
  - [ ] Sylvie → Cloak
  - [ ] Zombie Hunter Spidey → Right leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the The Watcher (What If...?) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
