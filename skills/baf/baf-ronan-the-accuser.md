# Complete the Ronan the Accuser Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Ronan the Accuser BAF from the 2007 Ronan the Accuser Wave wave. 8 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Ronan the Accuser** (2007, Ronan the Accuser Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Doctor Doom → Cape
  - [ ] Human Torch → Left leg
  - [ ] Invisible Woman → Torso
  - [ ] Mister Fantastic → Hammer
  - [ ] Mole Man → Left arm
  - [ ] Namor the Sub-Mariner → Right arm
  - [ ] Silver Surfer → Right leg
  - [ ] Thing → Head and helmet
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Ronan the Accuser torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
