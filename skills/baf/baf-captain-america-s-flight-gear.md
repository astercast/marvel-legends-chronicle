# Complete the Captain America's Flight Gear Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Captain America's Flight Gear BAF from the 2021 Captain America's Flight Gear Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Captain America's Flight Gear** (2021, Captain America's Flight Gear Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Helmut Zemo → Left wing tip
  - [ ] Loki → Redwing, jet pack and flight stand
  - [ ] Scarlet Witch → Right wing piece
  - [ ] U.S. Agent → Right wing tip
  - [ ] White Vision → Inner wing pieces
  - [ ] Winter Soldier → Left wing piece
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Captain America's Flight Gear torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
