# Complete the Brood Queen Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Brood Queen BAF from the 2007 Brood Queen Wave wave. 8 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Brood Queen** (2007, Brood Queen Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Black Knight (Dane Whitman) → Right legs
  - [ ] Bucky Barnes → Tail
  - [ ] Captain America → Left legs
  - [ ] Colossus → Right front leg
  - [ ] Cyclops → Head
  - [ ] Danger → Wings
  - [ ] Hydra Soldier → Left front leg
  - [ ] Marvel Girl (Rachel Summers) → Torso
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Brood Queen torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
