# Complete the Infinity Ultron (What If...?) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Infinity Ultron (What If...?) BAF from the 2022 Infinity Ultron Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Infinity Ultron (What If...?)** (2022, Infinity Ultron Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Hawkeye → Left arm
  - [ ] Kate Bishop → Right leg
  - [ ] Mr. Knight → Right arm
  - [ ] Ms. Marvel → Torso
  - [ ] Sharon Carter → Head and lance
  - [ ] She-Hulk → Left leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Infinity Ultron (What If...?) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
