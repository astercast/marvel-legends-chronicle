# Complete the Stilt-Man Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Stilt-Man BAF from the 2021 Stilt-Man Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Stilt-Man** (2021, Stilt-Man Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Frog-Man → Pistol, left arm and alternate hand
  - [ ] Hand Ninja → Leg extenders
  - [ ] Peter B. Parker → Head
  - [ ] Prowler (Aaron Davis) → Right arm, alternate hand and suitcase
  - [ ] Spider-Gwen and Spider-Ham → Torso
  - [ ] Spider-Man (Miles Morales) → Chest piece
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Stilt-Man torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
