# Complete the Juggernaut Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Juggernaut BAF from the 2016 Juggernaut Wave wave. 7 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Juggernaut** (2016, Juggernaut Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Cable → Lower torso
  - [ ] Havok → Right leg
  - [ ] Iceman → Left arm
  - [ ] Kitty Pryde and Lockheed → Right arm and Onslaught head for Red Onslaught BAF
  - [ ] Phoenix (Jean Grey) → Upper torso
  - [ ] Rogue → Left leg
  - [ ] Wolverine → Head
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Juggernaut torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
