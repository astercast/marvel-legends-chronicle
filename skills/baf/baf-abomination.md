# Complete the Abomination Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Abomination BAF from the 2016 Abomination Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Abomination** (2016, Abomination Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Captain America → Right arm
  - [ ] Captain Britain → Left arm
  - [ ] Eel (Edward Lavell) → Torso
  - [ ] Red Skull → Left leg
  - [ ] Scarlet Witch → Head
  - [ ] Wonder Man → Right leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Abomination torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Data references

- `data/figures.json`

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
