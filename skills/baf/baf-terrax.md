# Complete the Terrax Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Terrax BAF from the 2012 Terrax Wave (Return of Marvel Legends) wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Terrax** (2012, Terrax Wave (Return of Marvel Legends)).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Constrictor → Upper torso
  - [ ] Ghost Rider (Danny Ketch) → Left and right arms
  - [ ] Hope Summers → Head and axe
  - [ ] Iron Man → Right leg
  - [ ] Klaw → Left leg
  - [ ] Steve Rogers → Lower torso
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Terrax torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
