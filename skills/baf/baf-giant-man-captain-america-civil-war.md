# Complete the Giant-Man (Captain America: Civil War) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Giant-Man (Captain America: Civil War) BAF from the 2016 Giant-Man Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Giant-Man (Captain America: Civil War)** (2016, Giant-Man Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Black Panther → Left leg
  - [ ] Captain America → Head
  - [ ] Iron Man → Right arm
  - [ ] Nick Fury → Left arm
  - [ ] Nuke → Torso
  - [ ] Red Guardian → Right leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.
