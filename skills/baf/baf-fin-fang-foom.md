# Complete the Fin Fang Foom Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Fin Fang Foom BAF from the 2008 Fin Fang Foom Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Fin Fang Foom** (2008, Fin Fang Foom Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Absorbing Man → Right leg
  - [ ] Doc Samson → Lower torso
  - [ ] Hulk → Left leg, Left wing and arm, Tail
  - [ ] She-Hulk → Head and neck
  - [ ] Skaar → Upper torso
  - [ ] Wendigo → Right wing and arm
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Fin Fang Foom torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
