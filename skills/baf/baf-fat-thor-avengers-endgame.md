# Complete the Fat Thor (Avengers: Endgame) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Fat Thor (Avengers: Endgame) BAF from the 2019 Fat Thor Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Fat Thor (Avengers: Endgame)** (2019, Fat Thor Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Captain America → Left arm
  - [ ] Heimdall → Right leg
  - [ ] Iron Man → Right arm and alternate hand
  - [ ] Iron Patriot → Heads
  - [ ] Valkyrie → Right leg
  - [ ] Vision → Torso and Stormbreaker axe
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Fat Thor (Avengers: Endgame) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
