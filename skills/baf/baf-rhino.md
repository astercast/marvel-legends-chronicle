# Complete the Rhino Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Rhino BAF from the 2015 Rhino Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Rhino** (2015, Rhino Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Chameleon → Left arm
  - [ ] Ghost Rider (Johnny Blaze) → Left leg
  - [ ] Kraven the Hunter → Right leg
  - [ ] Misty Knight → Torso
  - [ ] Scarlet Spider (Ben Reilly) → Masked head and screaming head
  - [ ] Superior Venom → Right arm
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Rhino torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it — check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right — authenticate the figures first.
