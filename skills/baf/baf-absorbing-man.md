# Complete the Absorbing Man Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Absorbing Man BAF from the 2016 Absorbing Man Wave wave. 7 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Absorbing Man** (2016, Absorbing Man Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Beetle (Janice Lincoln) → Right leg
  - [ ] Jack O'Lantern → Left leg
  - [ ] Morbius, the Living Vampire → Left and right arms (stone and wood)
  - [ ] Speed Demon → Torso
  - [ ] Spider-Gwen → Wrecking ball
  - [ ] Spider-Man (Ben Reilly) → Left and right arms (steel and regular)
  - [ ] Venom → Two heads
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Absorbing Man torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
