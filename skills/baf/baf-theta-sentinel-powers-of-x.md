# Complete the Theta Sentinel (Powers of X) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Theta Sentinel (Powers of X) BAF from the 2021 Tri-Sentinel Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Theta Sentinel (Powers of X)** (2021, Tri-Sentinel Wave).
2. Checklist — buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Cyclops → Left arm
  - [ ] Jean Grey → Right leg
  - [ ] Magneto → Heads
  - [ ] Moira MacTaggert → Left leg
  - [ ] Omega Sentinel → Torso
  - [ ] Professor X → Right arm
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete — piece claims need proof.

Missing the Theta Sentinel (Powers of X) torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.
