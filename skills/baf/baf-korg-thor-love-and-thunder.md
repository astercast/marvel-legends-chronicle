# Complete the Korg (Thor: Love and Thunder) Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Korg (Thor: Love and Thunder) BAF from the 2022 Korg Wave wave. 6 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Korg (Thor: Love and Thunder)** (2022, Korg Wave).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Gorr → Right leg
  - [ ] King Valkyrie → Head and blaster
  - [ ] Ravager Thor → Left arm
  - [ ] Star-Lord → Right arm
  - [ ] Teen Groot → Torso
  - [ ] Thor (Jane Foster) → Left leg
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.
