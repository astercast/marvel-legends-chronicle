# Complete the Giant-Man Build-A-Figure

## Use when

You want to build the Giant-Man BAF from the 2006 Giant-Man (Walmart Exclusive) wave. 9 figures carry pieces.

## Inputs

- Your current figure inventory
- The BAF piece you still need

## Procedure

1. The BAF: **Giant-Man** (2006, Giant-Man (Walmart Exclusive)).
2. Checklist, buy the figure, keep the piece:
  - [ ] Ant-Man (Scott Lang) → Right arm
  - [ ] Captain Britain → Right leg
  - [ ] Havok → Left leg
  - [ ] Kitty Pryde and Lockheed → Upper torso and head
  - [ ] Sabretooth → Left foot
  - [ ] Sentry → Left arm
  - [ ] Thor → Right hand
  - [ ] Warbird (Carol Danvers) → Lower torso
  - [ ] Weapon X (Age of Apocalypse Wolverine) → Left hand, Right foot
3. Prioritize: figures you want anyway first, then cheapest piece-carriers.
4. Verify each piece against the BAF's assembly (some waves split one piece across variants).
5. Keep boxes until the BAF is complete, piece claims need proof.

## Worked example

Missing the Giant-Man torso? Find which figure above carries it, check sold listings for the cheapest loose complete figure.

## Pitfalls

- Buying a figure for the piece, then discovering the variant doesn't include it, check bafPiece notes.
- Bootleg BAF pieces don't fit right, authenticate the figures first.
