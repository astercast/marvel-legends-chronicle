# Complete a wave

## Use when

Wave completion projects.

## Inputs

- The wave
- Your owned list

## Procedure

1. Use the wave's BAF skill, it lists every piece-carrier.
2. Buy the BAF pieces first (they appreciate); fill commons last (they don't).
3. Lots > singles for commons, wave lots surface constantly.
4. Track in the Chronicle as you go.

## Worked example

Wave at 5/8: BAF pieces first ($90), commons as a lot ($40), done under budget.

## Data references

- `data/figures.json`

## Pitfalls

- Buying commons first, they're always available, BAF pieces aren't.
