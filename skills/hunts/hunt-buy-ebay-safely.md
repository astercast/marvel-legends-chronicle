# Buy safely on eBay

## Use when

Every eBay purchase.

## Inputs

- Your target + max price from sold data

## Procedure

1. Filter: sold listings for price, then active with 'Buy It Now + Best Offer'.
2. Offer 15–20% under ask on stale listings (30+ days).
3. Read the seller: feedback, other listings, photo originality.
4. Run the authenticate-a-listing skill on anything over $50.

## Worked example

Stale $80 listing → $65 offer accepted, sold data said $62.

## Pitfalls

- Bidding wars, set your max from solds and walk away at it.
