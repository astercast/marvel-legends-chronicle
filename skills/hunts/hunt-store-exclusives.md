# Hunt store exclusives

## Use when

Chasing retailer exclusives.

## Inputs

- The exclusive
- Retailer restock patterns

## Procedure

1. Learn the retailer's restock rhythm (Target: Tuesday mornings, etc.).
2. Brickseek-style stock checkers for in-store hunts.
3. Online: set alerts; exclusives sell out in waves, not once.

## Worked example

Target exclusive restocked three times in a month — the patient got it at retail.

## Pitfalls

- Paying scalper prices during wave one — waves two and three exist.
