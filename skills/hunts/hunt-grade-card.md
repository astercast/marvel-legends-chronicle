# Grade card condition

## Use when

Buying or selling carded figures.

## Inputs

- The carded figure, good light

## Procedure

1. Corners: sharp? Blunt one corner = a full grade down.
2. Edges: shelf wear along the bottom is the common flaw.
3. Bubble: clear? Yellowed? Cracked? Lifting?
4. Card: price stickers, bends, spine stress — photograph all of it.
5. Grade honestly: C10 is a myth; most 'mint' is C8.

## Worked example

C8.5 assessment: one soft corner, clear bubble — priced as C8, sold fast.

## Pitfalls

- Calling everything mint — buyers distrust perfect grades on imperfect cards.
