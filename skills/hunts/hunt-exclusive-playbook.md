# Hunt store and convention exclusives

## Use when

Chasing any retailer or convention exclusive, current or vintage.

## Inputs

- The exclusive
- Your max price

## Procedure

1. Know the venue's pattern. SDCC (booth-first, then Hasbro Pulse online): genuinely limited at the show, wider online, aftermarket spikes day-one, dips by September.
2. PulseCon (Hasbro's virtual con, est. 2020) is the accessible exclusive, no travel, retail price, ships to US/Canada/UK.
3. Target: Geek-Out/Geek Fest event drops; exclusives sell out in waves, not once, restocks happen. (2026: Target also cancelled confirmed preorders, order early AND have a backup.)
4. Walmart: Collector Con events with livestreams; Ares and Nemesis BAF waves were Walmart-exclusive waves, check these before assuming a BAF is common.
5. Walgreens: the Fantastic Four/Inhumans destination (Invisible Woman 2017, Human Torch, Mr. Fantastic, Thing, Namor, Medusa, Silver Surfer, Quasar, Binary, Sentry, Baron Zemo), limited store quantities, also on walgreens.com.
6. Amazon: Marvel vs. Capcom box sets (2025); Fan Channel (online specialty retailers): 20th Anniversary line, X-Force reissues.
7. The 2020 rule: Pulse-era (2020+) exclusives are produced to meet demand and often return or get reissued; pre-2020 con exclusives generally were not, and carry the real premiums.

## Worked example

Missed a PulseCon drop? Wait for the Pulse restock wave before touching the aftermarket, most Pulse-era exclusives come back.
