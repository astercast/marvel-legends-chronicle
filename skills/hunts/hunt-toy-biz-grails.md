# Hunt Toy Biz grails

## Use when

Vintage hunting.

## Inputs

- Your grail list with max prices

## Procedure

1. Flea markets, estate sales, and lots, grails hide in collections, not listings.
2. Learn the era's packaging at a glance (see the Toy Biz era skill).
3. Authenticate on the spot, bring the bootleg checklists.
4. Negotiate from sold data, loaded on your phone.

## Worked example

Flea-market Series 2 lot: $30 for five figures including a $50 grail, knowledge paid.

## Data references

- `data/encyclopedia.json`

## Pitfalls

- 'Vintage' pricing on common Toy Biz, most are $10–15 loose.
