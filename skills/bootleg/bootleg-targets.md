# Spot bootlegs: targets

## Use when

Authenticating a figure or listing, run all six bootleg skills, not just one.

## Inputs

- Clear photos (or the figure in hand)

## Procedure

1. Work the checklist, one fail doesn't convict, two corroborating fails do:
2.   - [ ] Dragon Man, Ronan, and the Juggernaut BAF recast are known KO targets
  - [ ] If a rare figure sells at a common price, be suspicious
3. Cross-check with the listing/price-logic skill, bootlegs cluster at impossible prices.

## Worked example

Blurry card printing + wrong plastic sheen = walk away, no matter the price.

## Data references

- `data/encyclopedia.json`

## Pitfalls

- Authenticating from one blurry photo, demand clear shots or pass.
