# Run the reissue radar on a grail

## Use when

Before paying a premium on any hall-of-fame-tier figure.

## Inputs

- The grail
- Its price evidence

## Procedure

1. Ask the one question: is the premium driven by RARITY (short-pack, prototype, limited con run, crowdfund cap) or AVAILABILITY (Hasbro just hasn't remade it yet)?
2. Rarity-based = resilient: unreleased prototypes (Crimson Dawn Psylocke), true Toy Biz chases, pre-2020 con exclusives, HasLab backer caps. Reissues can't touch these.
3. Availability-based = vulnerable: demand-driven premiums on reissuable sculpts (Deadpool S6, Sentinel BAF with HasLab existing, SDCC 2012 X-Force, which got a Fan Channel reissue in 2026). A reissue announcement collapses asking prices within days.
4. Check the Chronicle entry's reissue-radar flag, then verify yourself: has Hasbro reissued this sculpt/character recently? Is it in the current Retro program?
5. Documented mechanism: sellout → aftermarket spike → reissue (VHS Animated Series exclusives → Retro cards at $27.99, Sept 2026). Spike numbers are rarely published, don't pay spike prices.
6. Counter-proof: the 2018 Blue Wasp retro tribute was a 'middling seller, lingering on store shelves', true rarity is immune to reissues.

## Worked example

Grails with resilient radar: pay the premium with confidence. Vulnerable radar: set your max at the reissue price + small premium, or wait.

## Pitfalls

- Paying a 2021-style premium in a 2026 reissue environment, check the Retro program first.
- Treating 'sold out' as 'rare', sold out is a week, rare is forever.
