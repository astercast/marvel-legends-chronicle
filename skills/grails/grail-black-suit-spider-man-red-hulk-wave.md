# Research the Black Suit Spider-Man (Red Hulk wave) grail

## Use when

You're considering buying, selling, or hunting the Black Suit Spider-Man (Red Hulk wave) (Hasbro).

## Inputs

- The listing or lead
- Your authentication checklist

## Procedure

1. Why it matters: Target-exclusive wave figure; the definitive Black Suit ML for years.
2. Price evidence: ~$150 (UNVERIFIED, treat as collector lore, not market data).
3. Authenticate before negotiating, grails attract the best bootlegs.
4. Compare against 90 days of eBay SOLD listings, not asking prices.
5. For boxed grails: grade the card (see the card-grading skill) before paying a premium.

## Worked example

Listing claims a Black Suit Spider-Man (Red Hulk wave): run the bootleg checklist, pull 90-day solds, then negotiate from data, not excitement.
