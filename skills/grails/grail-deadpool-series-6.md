# Research the Deadpool (Series 6) grail

## Use when

You're considering buying, selling, or hunting the Deadpool (Series 6) (Toy Biz).

## Inputs

- The listing or lead
- Your authentication checklist

## Procedure

1. Why it matters: One of the three most-desired Toy Biz figures; peak-nostalgia wave.
2. Price evidence: ~$350 (UNVERIFIED, treat as collector lore, not market data).
3. Authenticate before negotiating, grails attract the best bootlegs.
4. Compare against 90 days of eBay SOLD listings, not asking prices.
5. For boxed grails: grade the card (see the card-grading skill) before paying a premium.

## Worked example

Listing claims a Deadpool (Series 6): run the bootleg checklist, pull 90-day solds, then negotiate from data, not excitement.
