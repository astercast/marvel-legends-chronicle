# Research the Phoenix / Dark Phoenix (Series 6) grail

## Use when

You're considering buying, selling, or hunting the Phoenix / Dark Phoenix (Series 6) (Toy Biz).

## Inputs

- The listing or lead
- Your authentication checklist

## Procedure

1. Why it matters: Fan-favorite sculpt with rare variants; Hasbro later revisited her in the Queen Brood wave.
2. Price evidence: Community claim (UNVERIFIED — treat as collector lore, not market data).
3. Authenticate before negotiating — grails attract the best bootlegs.
4. Compare against 90 days of eBay SOLD listings, not asking prices.
5. For boxed grails: grade the card (see the card-grading skill) before paying a premium.

## Worked example

Listing claims a Phoenix / Dark Phoenix (Series 6): run the bootleg checklist, pull 90-day solds, then negotiate from data, not excitement.

## Pitfalls

- Paying asking-price logic on a grail — solds are the market, asks are wishes.
- Skipping authentication because 'the seller seems nice.'
