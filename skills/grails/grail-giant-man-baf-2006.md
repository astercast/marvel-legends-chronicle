# Research the Giant-Man BAF (2006) grail

## Use when

You're considering buying, selling, or hunting the Giant-Man BAF (2006) (Toy Biz).

## Inputs

- The listing or lead
- Your authentication checklist

## Procedure

1. Why it matters: Late-line BAF; hard to complete as loose parts go missing.
2. Price evidence: ~$200 complete (UNVERIFIED, treat as collector lore, not market data).
3. Authenticate before negotiating, grails attract the best bootlegs.
4. Compare against 90 days of eBay SOLD listings, not asking prices.
5. For boxed grails: grade the card (see the card-grading skill) before paying a premium.

## Worked example

Listing claims a Giant-Man BAF (2006): run the bootleg checklist, pull 90-day solds, then negotiate from data, not excitement.

- `data/encyclopedia.json`

## Pitfalls

- Paying asking-price logic on a grail, solds are the market, asks are wishes.
- Skipping authentication because 'the seller seems nice.'
