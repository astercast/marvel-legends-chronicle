# Identify the packaging era

## Use when

Dating loose-card finds, spotting repacks, or authenticating Toy Biz-era figures.

## Inputs

- The packaging (photos or in hand)

## Procedure

1. Clamshell + 32-page comic pack-in + display stand = Toy Biz, 2002–2006. International Toy Biz may carry a poster instead of a comic (UK/CA 'Gold Poster' variants exist).
2. No comic pack-in, blister card = Hasbro takeover onward, 2007+.
3. Scooped-side window box with blue highlight side art and wave checklist on back = Infinite Series relaunch, 2013/14 onward.
4. Printed image instead of a window, comic-art box design = windowless/plastic-free era, 2022–2023. Retro line was exempt — a Retro card in this window is not an anomaly.
5. Bio-PET/recycled-PET window = late 2023 onward.
6. Toy Biz 90s-style cardback = Spider-Man: The Animated Series retro cards (2023) or the 20th Anniversary Fan Channel line (2022) — NOT original Toy Biz.
7. Packaging-era mismatch with the figure's release year is a repack/bootleg red flag.

## Worked example

Clamshell with comic: Toy Biz era confirmed — run the Toy Biz authentication checks next.

## Pitfalls

- Retro cards are homages, not vintage — don't pay vintage prices for a 2023 retro card.
