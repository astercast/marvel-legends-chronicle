# Collect every Winter Soldier

## Use when

You collect Winter Soldier. 9 releases are catalogued from 2010 to 2022.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2010 — Toys "R" Us 2-Packs: Winter Soldier (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2010 — Toys "R" Us 2-Packs: Winter Soldier [Red star on shoulder] (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2014 — Mandroid Wave (Infinite Series): Winter Soldier
  - 2016 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Winter Soldier (Walmart)
  - 2017 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Winter Soldier
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Winter Soldier (Target)
  - 2020 — Crimson Dynamo Wave: Winter Soldier
  - 2021 — Captain America's Flight Gear Wave: Winter Soldier
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Winter Soldier
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Winter Soldier run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
