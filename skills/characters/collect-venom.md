# Collect every Venom

## Use when

You collect Venom. 16 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004 — Box Set - Spider-Man vs. Sinister 6: Venom (Box set exclusive)
  - 2006 — Icons Series 2: Venom
  - 2006 — Icons Series 2: Venom [Unmasked (Eddie Brock)]
  - 2007 — Sandman (Spider-Man 3) Wave: Venom
  - 2016 — Absorbing Man Wave: Venom
  - 2018 — Monster Venom Wave: Venom
  - 2020 — Venompool Wave (Gamerverse): Venom
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Venom
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Venom (Hasbro PulseCon)
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Venom (Amazon)
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Venom
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Venom (Flash Thompson) (Target)
  - 2024 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Venom (Walmart)
  - 2025 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Venom
  - …and 2 more
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Venom run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
