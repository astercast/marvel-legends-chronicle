# Collect every Scarlet Witch

## Use when

You collect Scarlet Witch. 10 releases are catalogued from 2005 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2005 — Legendary Riders: Scarlet Witch
  - 2015 — Allfather Wave: Scarlet Witch
  - 2016 — Abomination Wave: Scarlet Witch
  - 2017 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Scarlet Witch
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Scarlet Witch (Entertainment Earth)
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Scarlet Witch (Amazon)
  - 2021 — Captain America's Flight Gear Wave: Scarlet Witch
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Scarlet Witch
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Scarlet Witch (Target)
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Scarlet Witch (Amazon)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Scarlet Witch run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
