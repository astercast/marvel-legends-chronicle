# Collect every Hawkeye

## Use when

You collect Hawkeye. 12 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004 — Series 7: Hawkeye
  - 2004 — Series 7: Hawkeye [Knuckle guard bow]
  - 2013 — Rocket Raccoon Wave: Hawkeye
  - 2015 — Allfather Wave: Hawkeye
  - 2015 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Hawkeye (Amazon)
  - 2016 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Hawkeye (Kate Bishop) (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2016 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Hawkeye
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Hawkeye (Target)
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Hawkeye
  - 2022 — Infinity Ultron Wave: Hawkeye
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Hawkeye
  - 2026 — Box Wave: Hawkeye (Bullseye)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Hawkeye run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
