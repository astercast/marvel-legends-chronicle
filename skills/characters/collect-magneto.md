# Collect every Magneto

## Use when

You collect Magneto. 12 releases are catalogued from 2002 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2002 — Series 3: Magneto
  - 2003 — Box Set - X-Men Legends: Magneto
  - 2007 — Icons Series 7: Magneto
  - 2014 — Jubilee Wave (Toys "R" Us Exclusive): Magneto (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2018 — Apocalypse Wave: Magneto
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Magneto (Amazon)
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Magneto
  - 2021 — Tri-Sentinel Wave: Magneto
  - 2021 — Colossus Wave (Age of Apocalypse): Magneto
  - 2023 — X-Men '97 Wave 1: Magneto
  - 2024 — X-Men '97 Wave 2: Magneto
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Magneto (Amazon)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Magneto run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
