# Collect every Green Goblin

## Use when

You collect Green Goblin. 9 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004 — Box Set - Spider-Man vs. Sinister 6: Green Goblin
  - 2006 — Series 13 - Onslaught: Green Goblin
  - 2006 — Series 13 - Onslaught: Green Goblin [Unmasked Norman Osborn]
  - 2007 — Sandman (Spider-Man 3) Wave: Green Goblin
  - 2017 — Sandman Wave: Green Goblin
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Green Goblin
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Green Goblin (Hasbro Pulse)
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Green Goblin
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Green Goblin
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Green Goblin run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
