# Collect every Wolverine

## Use when

You collect Wolverine. 56 releases are catalogued from 2002 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2002, Series 3: Wolverine
  - 2002, Series 3: Wolverine [Unmasked]
  - 2003, Box Set - X-Men Legends: Wolverine
  - 2004, Series 6: Wolverine
  - 2004, Series 6: Wolverine [Unmasked]
  - 2004, Series 7: Wolverine
  - 2005, Legendary Riders: Wolverine
  - 2005, Legendary Riders: Wolverine [Younger Logan, white T-shirt/blue pants, cowboy hat]
  - 2005, Series 12 - Apocalypse: Wolverine
  - 2005, Series 12 - Apocalypse: Wolverine [Unmasked]
  - 2006, Face-Off Series 2: Wolverine
  - 2006, Face-Off Series 2: Wolverine [Screaming]
  - 2006, Icons Series 1: Wolverine
  - 2006, Icons Series 1: Wolverine [Unmasked]
  - …and 42 more
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Wolverine run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
