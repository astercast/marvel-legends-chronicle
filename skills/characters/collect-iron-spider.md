# Collect every Iron Spider

## Use when

You collect Iron Spider. 8 releases are catalogued from 2018 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2018, Thanos Wave: Iron Spider
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Iron Spider
  - 2019, Store & Convention Exclusives: Iron Spider
  - 2020, Store & Convention Exclusives: Iron Spider
  - 2022, Store & Convention Exclusives: Iron Spider
  - 2024, Store & Convention Exclusives: Iron Spider
  - 2025, Store & Convention Exclusives: Iron Spider (Mary Jane Watson)
  - 2026, Store & Convention Exclusives: Iron Spider (Aaron Davis)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Iron Spider run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
