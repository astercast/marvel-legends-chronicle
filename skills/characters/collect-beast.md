# Collect every Beast

## Use when

You collect Beast. 12 releases are catalogued from 2003 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2003 — Series 4: Beast
  - 2003 — Box Set - X-Men Legends: Beast
  - 2006 — Icons Series 3: Beast
  - 2006 — Icons Series 3: Beast [Blue, lab coat and glasses]
  - 2007 — Annihilus Wave: Beast
  - 2009 — Nemesis Wave (Walmart Exclusive): Beast (Walmart)
  - 2014 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Beast (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2019 — Caliban Wave: Beast
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Beast (Fan channel)
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Beast
  - 2025 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Beast
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Beast
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Beast run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
