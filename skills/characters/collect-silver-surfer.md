# Collect every Silver Surfer

## Use when

You collect Silver Surfer. 9 releases are catalogued from 2007 to 2025.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2007 — Ronan the Accuser Wave: Silver Surfer
  - 2007 — Store Exclusives: Silver Surfer (Walmart)
  - 2007 — Icons Series 6: Silver Surfer
  - 2007 — Icons (Hasbro 12-inch) — additional releases: Silver Surfer [Cosmic] (Target)
  - 2008 — Red Hulk Wave (Target Exclusive): Silver Surfer (Target)
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Silver Surfer (Walgreens)
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Silver Surfer (HasLab)
  - 2025 — Fantastic Four: First Steps Wave: Silver Surfer (Shalla-Bal)
  - 2025 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Silver Surfer
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Silver Surfer run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
