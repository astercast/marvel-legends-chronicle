# Collect every She-Hulk

## Use when

You collect She-Hulk. 10 releases are catalogued from 2007 to 2025.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2007 — Blob Wave: She-Hulk
  - 2007 — San Diego Comic-Con Exclusives: She-Hulk (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2008 — Fin Fang Foom Wave: She-Hulk
  - 2008 — SDCC Box Set - Fin Fang Foom: She-Hulk (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2017 — Store & Convention Exclusives: She-Hulk (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2020 — Super-Skrull Wave: She-Hulk
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: She-Hulk (Fan channel)
  - 2022 — Infinity Ultron Wave: She-Hulk
  - 2024 — Store & Convention Exclusives: She-Hulk
  - 2025 — Store & Convention Exclusives: She-Hulk
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a She-Hulk run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
