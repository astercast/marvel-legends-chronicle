# Collect every Captain Marvel

## Use when

You collect Captain Marvel. 11 releases are catalogued from 2006 to 2023.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2006 — Series 15 - MODOK: Captain Marvel (Mar-Vell)
  - 2006 — Series 15 - MODOK: Captain Marvel (Genis-Vell) [Genis-Vell]
  - 2015 — Allfather Wave: Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers)
  - 2017 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers) (Marvel Unlimited)
  - 2019 — Kree Sentry Wave: Captain Marvel (Genis-Vell)
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Captain Marvel (Target)
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Captain Marvel (Walmart)
  - 2020 — Abomination Wave (Gamerverse): Captain Marvel (Mar-Vell)
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Captain Marvel (Amazon)
  - 2023 — Hulk (Amadeus Cho) Wave: Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers)
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Captain Marvel (Monica Rambeau)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Captain Marvel run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
