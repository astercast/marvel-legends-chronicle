# Collect every Daredevil

## Use when

You collect Daredevil. 22 releases are catalogued from 2002 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2002 — Series 3: Daredevil
  - 2003 — Box Set - Urban Legends: Daredevil
  - 2006 — Face-Off Series 1: Daredevil
  - 2006 — Face-Off Series 1: Daredevil [Unmasked]
  - 2007 — Icons Series 5: Daredevil [Red costume]
  - 2007 — Icons Series 5: Daredevil [Yellow costume]
  - 2009 — Nemesis Wave (Walmart Exclusive): Daredevil (Walmart)
  - 2009 — Nemesis Wave (Walmart Exclusive): Daredevil [Red] (Walmart)
  - 2015 — Hobgoblin Wave: Daredevil
  - 2015 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Daredevil (Walgreens)
  - 2017 — Man-Thing Wave (Netflix): Daredevil
  - 2018 — SP//dr Wave: Daredevil
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Daredevil (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Daredevil
  - …and 8 more
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Daredevil run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
