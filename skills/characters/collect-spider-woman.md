# Collect every Spider-Woman

## Use when

You collect Spider-Woman. 9 releases are catalogued from 2006 to 2025.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2006, Series 15 - MODOK: Spider-Woman (Jessica Drew)
  - 2006, Series 15 - MODOK: Spider-Woman (Julia Carpenter) [Julia Carpenter (Arachne)]
  - 2015, Thanos Wave: Spider-Woman (Jessica Drew)
  - 2018, Lizard Wave: Spider-Woman (Jessica Drew)
  - 2019, Molten Man Wave: Spider-Woman (Julia Carpenter)
  - 2023, Across the Spider-Verse Wave 1: Spider-Woman (Jessica Drew)
  - 2023, Store & Convention Exclusives: Spider-Woman (Julia Carpenter) (Amazon)
  - 2023, Store & Convention Exclusives: Spider-Woman (Jessica Drew)
  - 2025, Store & Convention Exclusives: Spider-Woman (Jessica Drew) (Marvel Unlimited)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Spider-Woman run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
