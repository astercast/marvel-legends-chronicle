# Collect every Storm

## Use when

You collect Storm. 13 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2025.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004 — Series 8: Storm
  - 2004 — Series 8: Storm [Mohawk]
  - 2014 — Jubilee Wave (Toys "R" Us Exclusive): Storm (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2018 — Apocalypse Wave: Storm
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm (Target)
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm (Fan channel)
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm (Hasbro Pulse)
  - 2023 — X-Men '97 Wave 1: Storm
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm
  - 2025 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm (Target)
  - 2025 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Storm (Hasbro Pulse)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Storm run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
