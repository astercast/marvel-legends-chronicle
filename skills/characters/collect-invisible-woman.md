# Collect every Invisible Woman

## Use when

You collect Invisible Woman. 10 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004 — Box Set - Fantastic Four: Invisible Woman (Box set exclusive)
  - 2004 — Box Set - Fantastic Four: Invisible Woman [Translucent] (Box set exclusive)
  - 2007 — Ronan the Accuser Wave: Invisible Woman
  - 2009 — 2-Packs Series 2: Invisible Woman
  - 2020 — Super-Skrull Wave: Invisible Woman
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Invisible Woman
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Invisible Woman (Hasbro Pulse)
  - 2025 — Fantastic Four: First Steps Wave: Invisible Woman
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Invisible Woman
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Invisible Woman (Amazon)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Invisible Woman run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
