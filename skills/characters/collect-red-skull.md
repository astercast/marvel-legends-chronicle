# Collect every Red Skull

## Use when

You collect Red Skull. 8 releases are catalogued from 2003 to 2022.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2003, Series 5: Red Skull
  - 2006, Face-Off Series 1: Red Skull
  - 2014, Mandroid Wave (Infinite Series): Red Skull
  - 2016, Abomination Wave: Red Skull
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Red Skull (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Red Skull
  - 2021, Xemnu Wave: Red Skull
  - 2022, Khonshu Wave: Red Skull
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Red Skull run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
