# Collect every Gambit

## Use when

You collect Gambit. 9 releases are catalogued from 2003 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2003, Series 4: Gambit
  - 2003, Box Set - X-Men Legends: Gambit
  - 2019, Caliban Wave: Gambit
  - 2020, Store & Convention Exclusives: Gambit (Target)
  - 2023, X-Men '97 Wave 1: Gambit
  - 2023, Store & Convention Exclusives: Gambit
  - 2025, Nemesis Wave: Gambit
  - 2025, Store & Convention Exclusives: Gambit (Hasbro Pulse)
  - 2026, Avengers: Doomsday Wave 1: Gambit
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Gambit run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
