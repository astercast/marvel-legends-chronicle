# Collect every Sabretooth

## Use when

You collect Sabretooth. 8 releases are catalogued from 2003 to 2024.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2003, Series 5: Sabretooth
  - 2006, Giant-Man (Walmart Exclusive): Sabretooth (Walmart)
  - 2006, Face-Off Series 2: Sabretooth
  - 2013, Puck Wave (Hasbro Previews Exclusive): Sabretooth (Hasbro Previews)
  - 2018, Apocalypse Wave: Sabretooth
  - 2021, Colossus Wave (Age of Apocalypse): Sabretooth
  - 2022, Bonebreaker Wave: Sabretooth
  - 2024, Store & Convention Exclusives: Sabretooth
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Sabretooth run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
