# Collect every Professor X

## Use when

You collect Professor X. 8 releases are catalogued from 2005 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2005 — Series 9 - Galactus: Professor X
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Professor X
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Professor X (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Professor X
  - 2021 — Tri-Sentinel Wave: Professor X
  - 2024 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Professor X
  - 2025 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Professor X (Target)
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Professor X (San Diego Comic-Con)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Professor X run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
