# Collect every Iron Man

## Use when

You collect Iron Man. 77 releases are catalogued from 2002 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2002, Series 1: Iron Man
  - 2002, Series 1: Iron Man [Gold Model 2 (horned) version]
  - 2002, Series 1: Iron Man [Stealth armor]
  - 2002, Walmart Exclusives: Iron Man [Stealth armor] (Walmart)
  - 2004, Series 7: Iron Man
  - 2004, Series 8: Iron Man
  - 2005, Legendary Riders: Iron Man
  - 2006, Series 14 - Mojo: Iron Man
  - 2006, Series 14 - Mojo: Iron Man [Gold]
  - 2006, Series 15 - MODOK: Iron Man
  - 2006, Box Set - House of M: Iron Man
  - 2006, Face-Off Series 2: Iron Man
  - 2006, Icons Series 1: Iron Man
  - 2006, Icons Series 1: Iron Man [Gold]
  - …and 63 more
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Iron Man run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
