# Collect every Mister Fantastic

## Use when

You collect Mister Fantastic. 9 releases are catalogued from 2003 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2003 — Series 5: Mister Fantastic
  - 2004 — Box Set - Fantastic Four: Mister Fantastic
  - 2007 — Ronan the Accuser Wave: Mister Fantastic
  - 2008 — 2-Packs Series 1: Mister Fantastic
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Mister Fantastic (Walgreens)
  - 2020 — Super-Skrull Wave: Mister Fantastic
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Mister Fantastic
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Mister Fantastic
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Mister Fantastic (Walmart)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Mister Fantastic run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
