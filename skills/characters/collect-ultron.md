# Collect every Ultron

## Use when

You collect Ultron. 8 releases are catalogued from 2005 to 2023.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2005 — Legendary Riders: Ultron
  - 2013 — Iron Monger Wave: Ultron
  - 2013 — Convention & Mail-Away Exclusives: Ultron (Marvel Unlimited)
  - 2015 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ultron (Target)
  - 2015 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ultron (Disney Store)
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ultron (GameStop)
  - 2021 — Ursa Major Wave: Ultron
  - 2023 — Cassie Lang Wave: Ultron
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Ultron run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
