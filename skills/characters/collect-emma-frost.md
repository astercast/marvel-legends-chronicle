# Collect every Emma Frost

## Use when

You collect Emma Frost. 8 releases are catalogued from 2007 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2007 — Annihilus Wave: Emma Frost
  - 2007 — Store Exclusives: Emma Frost (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2013 — Puck Wave (Hasbro Previews Exclusive): Emma Frost (Hasbro Previews)
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Emma Frost (Walgreens)
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Emma Frost (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2023 — Ch'od Wave: Emma Frost
  - 2025 — X-Men '97 Wave 3: Emma Frost
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Emma Frost (Target)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Emma Frost run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
