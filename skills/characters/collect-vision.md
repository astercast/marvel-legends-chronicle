# Collect every Vision

## Use when

You collect Vision. 11 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004, Series 7: Vision
  - 2004, Series 7: Vision [Phasing (translucent)]
  - 2008, Ares Wave (Walmart Exclusive): Vision (Walmart)
  - 2015, Hulkbuster Wave: Vision
  - 2015, Store & Convention Exclusives: Vision (Target)
  - 2016, Store & Convention Exclusives: Vision (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Vision (Entertainment Earth)
  - 2019, Fat Thor Wave: Vision
  - 2021, Store & Convention Exclusives: Vision
  - 2024, The Void Wave: Vision
  - 2026, Store & Convention Exclusives: Vision (Amazon)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Vision run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
