# Collect every Ant-Man

## Use when

You collect Ant-Man. 11 releases are catalogued from 2006 to 2023.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2006 — Giant-Man (Walmart Exclusive): Ant-Man (Scott Lang) (Walmart)
  - 2015 — Ultron Prime Wave: Ant-Man
  - 2015 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ant-Man (Scott Lang) (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2015 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ant-Man (Hank Pym) (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2018 — Cull Obsidian Wave: Ant-Man
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ant-Man (Scott Lang)
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ant-Man
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ant-Man
  - 2021 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ant-Man (Hank Pym)
  - 2023 — Cassie Lang Wave: Ant-Man (Scott Lang)
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Ant-Man (Scott Lang) (Target)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Ant-Man run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
