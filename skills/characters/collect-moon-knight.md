# Collect every Moon Knight

## Use when

You collect Moon Knight. 9 releases are catalogued from 2006 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2006 — Series 15 - MODOK: Moon Knight
  - 2006 — Series 15 - MODOK: Moon Knight [Silver]
  - 2017 — Vulture Wave: Moon Knight
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Moon Knight (Walgreens)
  - 2022 — Infinity Ultron Wave: Moon Knight
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Moon Knight (Target)
  - 2023 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Moon Knight (Fan channel)
  - 2024 — Blackheart Wave (Strange Tales): Moon Knight
  - 2026 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Moon Knight
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Moon Knight run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
