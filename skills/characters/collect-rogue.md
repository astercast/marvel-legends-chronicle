# Collect every Rogue

## Use when

You collect Rogue. 10 releases are catalogued from 2003 to 2025.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2003, Box Set - X-Men Legends: Rogue (Box set exclusive)
  - 2016, Juggernaut Wave: Rogue
  - 2020, Store & Convention Exclusives: Rogue (Target)
  - 2020, Store & Convention Exclusives: Rogue
  - 2021, Colossus Wave (Age of Apocalypse): Rogue
  - 2023, X-Men '97 Wave 1: Rogue
  - 2023, Store & Convention Exclusives: Rogue
  - 2024, Store & Convention Exclusives: Rogue (Amazon)
  - 2025, Store & Convention Exclusives: Rogue (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2025, Store & Convention Exclusives: Rogue (Hasbro Pulse)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Rogue run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
