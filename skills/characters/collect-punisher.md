# Collect every Punisher

## Use when

You collect Punisher. 21 releases are catalogued from 2003 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2003, Series 4: Punisher
  - 2003, Series 4: Punisher [Black belt]
  - 2003, Box Set - Urban Legends: Punisher
  - 2004, Series 6: Punisher
  - 2006, Face-Off Series 2: Punisher
  - 2006, Face-Off Series 2: Punisher [Modern]
  - 2007, Icons Series 5: Punisher
  - 2009, Nemesis Wave (Walmart Exclusive): Punisher (Walmart)
  - 2009, Nemesis Wave (Walmart Exclusive): Punisher [Camo (Punisher 2005 video game)] (Walmart)
  - 2013, Epic Heroes: Punisher
  - 2013, Epic Heroes: Punisher [Thunderbolts red logo]
  - 2016, Store & Convention Exclusives: Punisher (Walgreens)
  - 2017, Man-Thing Wave (Netflix): Punisher
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Punisher (Fan channel)
  - …and 7 more
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Punisher run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
