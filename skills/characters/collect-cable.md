# Collect every Cable

## Use when

You collect Cable. 8 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2025.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004 — Series 6: Cable
  - 2004 — Series 6: Cable [Brown pants]
  - 2007 — Store Exclusives: Cable (Walmart)
  - 2016 — Juggernaut Wave: Cable
  - 2018 — Sasquatch Wave: Cable
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Cable (Walmart)
  - 2024 — Zabu Wave: Cable
  - 2025 — X-Men '97 Wave 3: Cable
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Cable run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
