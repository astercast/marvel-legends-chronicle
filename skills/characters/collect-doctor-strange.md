# Collect every Doctor Strange

## Use when

You collect Doctor Strange. 11 releases are catalogued from 2005 to 2024.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2005, Series 9 - Galactus: Doctor Strange
  - 2015, Hulkbuster Wave: Doctor Strange
  - 2015, Store & Convention Exclusives: Doctor Strange (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2016, Dormammu Wave: Doctor Strange
  - 2016, Dormammu Wave: Doctor Strange
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Doctor Strange
  - 2021, Armadillo Wave: Doctor Strange
  - 2022, Rintrah Wave: Doctor Strange
  - 2022, Store & Convention Exclusives: Doctor Strange (Walmart)
  - 2024, Store & Convention Exclusives: Doctor Strange (Hasbro Pulse)
  - 2024, Store & Convention Exclusives: Doctor Strange (Amazon)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Doctor Strange run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
