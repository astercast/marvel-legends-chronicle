# Collect every Loki

## Use when

You collect Loki. 9 releases are catalogued from 2006 to 2022.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2006 — Series 13 - Onslaught: Loki
  - 2006 — Series 13 - Onslaught: Loki [Crown of Lies]
  - 2016 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Loki
  - 2017 — Gladiator Hulk Wave: Loki
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Loki
  - 2019 — Smart Hulk Wave: Loki
  - 2019 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Loki (Walmart)
  - 2021 — Captain America's Flight Gear Wave: Loki
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Loki
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Loki run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
