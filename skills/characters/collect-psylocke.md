# Collect every Psylocke

## Use when

You collect Psylocke. 9 releases are catalogued from 2006 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2006, Series 14 - Mojo: Psylocke
  - 2012, SDCC Box Set - Uncanny X-Force: Psylocke (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2018, Apocalypse Wave: Psylocke
  - 2020, Store & Convention Exclusives: Psylocke (Amazon)
  - 2023, Store & Convention Exclusives: Psylocke
  - 2024, Store & Convention Exclusives: Psylocke
  - 2025, Store & Convention Exclusives: Psylocke (Target)
  - 2026, Box Wave: Psylocke
  - 2026, Store & Convention Exclusives: Psylocke
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Psylocke run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.
