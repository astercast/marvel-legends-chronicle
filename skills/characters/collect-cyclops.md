# Collect every Cyclops

## Use when

You collect Cyclops. 19 releases are catalogued from 2005 to 2026.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2005, Series 10 - Sentinel: Cyclops
  - 2005, Series 10 - Sentinel: Cyclops [X-Factor costume]
  - 2007, Brood Queen Wave: Cyclops
  - 2007, Icons Series 7: Cyclops
  - 2013, Puck Wave (Hasbro Previews Exclusive): Cyclops (Hasbro Previews)
  - 2014, Jubilee Wave (Toys "R" Us Exclusive): Cyclops (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2014, Store & Convention Exclusives: Cyclops (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2017, Warlock Wave: Cyclops
  - 2017, Store & Convention Exclusives: Cyclops (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Cyclops
  - 2021, Tri-Sentinel Wave: Cyclops
  - 2021, Colossus Wave (Age of Apocalypse): Cyclops
  - 2021, Store & Convention Exclusives: Cyclops
  - 2023, Ch'od Wave: Cyclops
  - …and 5 more
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Cyclops run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
