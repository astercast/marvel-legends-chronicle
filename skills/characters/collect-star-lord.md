# Collect every Star-Lord

## Use when

You collect Star-Lord. 11 releases are catalogued from 2014 to 2023.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically, oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2014, Groot Wave: Star-Lord
  - 2014, Store & Convention Exclusives: Star-Lord (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2015, Store & Convention Exclusives: Star-Lord (Entertainment Earth)
  - 2017, Titus Wave: Star-Lord
  - 2017, Mantis Wave: Star-Lord
  - 2017, Store & Convention Exclusives: Star-Lord
  - 2018, Store & Convention Exclusives: Star-Lord
  - 2021, The Watcher Wave: Star-Lord (T'Challa)
  - 2022, Korg Wave: Star-Lord
  - 2023, Cosmo the Spacedog Wave: Star-Lord
  - 2023, Store & Convention Exclusives: Star-Lord (Walmart)
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Star-Lord run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes, some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
