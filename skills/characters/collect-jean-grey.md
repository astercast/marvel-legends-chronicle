# Collect every Jean Grey

## Use when

You collect Jean Grey. 17 releases are catalogued from 2004 to 2025.

## Inputs

- Your current holdings
- Budget tier (loose / boxed / graded)

## Procedure

1. Work the list chronologically — oldest first, they appreciate fastest:
2.   - 2004 — Series 6: Jean Grey
  - 2004 — Series 6: Jean Grey [Dark Phoenix]
  - 2007 — Blob Wave: Jean Grey
  - 2007 — Blob Wave: Jean Grey [Phoenix]
  - 2007 — Store Exclusives: Jean Grey (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2007 — Store Exclusives: Jean Grey (Walmart)
  - 2013 — Rocket Raccoon Wave: Jean Grey
  - 2017 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Jean Grey (Toys "R" Us)
  - 2018 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Jean Grey
  - 2020 — Sugar Man Wave: Jean Grey
  - 2020 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Jean Grey (San Diego Comic-Con)
  - 2021 — Tri-Sentinel Wave: Jean Grey
  - 2022 — Store & Convention Exclusives: Jean Grey (Hasbro Pulse)
  - 2024 — X-Men '97 Wave 2: Jean Grey
  - …and 3 more
3. Mark owned/wanted in the Chronicle tracker as you go.
4. Toy Biz-era releases: authenticate hard (bootlegs concentrate here).
5. Exclusives and variants: decide your completion rule NOW (all variants? one per sculpt?).

## Worked example

Starting a Jean Grey run? Grab the most recent mainline release first (cheapest, in-stock), then hunt backwards by price.

## Pitfalls

- No completion rule = endless spending. Define 'complete' before you start.
- Variant rabbit holes — some characters have 5+ releases of one sculpt.
