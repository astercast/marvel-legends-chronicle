# Find your collector people

## Use when

Building your community circle.

## Inputs

- Your collecting lane

## Procedure

1. Search hashtags for your lane (#xmenlegends, #spideyshelf).
2. Follow 10, engage genuinely for two weeks — comments, not just likes.
3. Trade with established members before strangers.

## Worked example

X-Men collector: #xmenlegends → 10 follows → first trade in week three.

## Pitfalls

- DMing 'sell me that' as a first message — engage first.
