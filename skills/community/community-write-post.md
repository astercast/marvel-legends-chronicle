# Write a community post

## Use when

Sharing pickups, shelfies, and hot takes.

## Inputs

- Your photos
- The story behind the figure

## Procedure

1. Lead with the photo, cropped, lit, eye-level.
2. One hook line, then the story: why THIS figure.
3. Tag the figure so it links to its Chronicle entry.
4. Ask one question, posts with questions get 3× the replies.

## Worked example

'Finally completed the Sentinel BAF, which piece took you longest?' + build photo.

## Pitfalls

- Photo-dump with no words, the algorithm and the humans both skip it.
