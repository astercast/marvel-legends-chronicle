# Photograph your figures

## Use when

Community posts, sale listings, insurance records.

## Inputs

- Phone
- One lamp
- Neutral background

## Procedure

1. One key light at 45°, neutral background (poster board works).
2. Eye-level with the figure, never shoot down at toys.
3. For sales: front, back, joints, flaws, honest photos sell faster.
4. For shelfies: depth, stagger figures, blur the background.

## Worked example

Sale listing: 6 photos (front/back/left/right/joints/flaw close-up), sells in days, not weeks.

## Pitfalls

- Flash on-camera, flattens everything and blows out paint.
