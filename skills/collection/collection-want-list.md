# Build a want list that works

## Use when

Turning 'I want everything' into an actionable plan.

## Inputs

- Your focus characters or eras

## Procedure

1. List wants with a max price each, a want without a price is a wish.
2. Rank by: grail risk (buy appreciating assets first) vs availability.
3. Review monthly; kill wants you've outgrown.

## Worked example

Want: Sentinel BAF, max $180, priority 1 (appreciating, complete the wave).

## Data references

- `data/figures.json`

## Pitfalls

- An infinite want list, cap it at 20, finish it, then add more.
