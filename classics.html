The Classics family

Toy Biz, 2001 to 2006, 143 figures in 30 waves

The Classics lines are the Toy Biz sister lines that grew up beside Marvel Legends: Spider-Man Classics and its successor Spider-Man Classic, X-Men Classics, Hulk Classics, and Fantastic Four Classics. They are a separate census family in this Chronicle. Their counts stand apart from the Marvel Legends census of 1,949 figures in 170 waves and are never merged into it.

3 of the 143 Classics figures carry a photograph so far. Pictures join this family only from lawful retailer photography of the kind the main census uses; the rest are absence by design, the same standard the main line applies to its own unpictured figures.
