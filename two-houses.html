The line, under its makers

The Two Houses.

Marvel Legends has had exactly two makers. Toy Biz opened the line in 2002 and closed its account in 2006; Hasbro opened in 2007 and carries the line still. This ledger files every figure in the catalog under the house that made it, its waves, its builds, its characters and debuts, the exclusives and variants its record names, and it reports the seam between the houses exactly where the catalog puts it: the two houses share no year. A character is counted by the archive's own rule, the name with any parenthetical removed, and a character debuts under whichever house made its first figure.

The succession

Counted in this browser from the catalog itself (1,939 figures). A house is a distinct maker as the catalog records it, the same field the wave pages carry, and the two records agree on every wave. An exclusive is a figure whose record names an exclusive channel; a variant is a figure whose record names one; a figure the catalog leaves unmarked is counted as unmarked, never reclassified by hand. This desk keeps no prices: a makers' ledger is the record, not the market. For the eras the houses span, walk the Archive Tour; for each character's first figure under either house, read First Appearances.
