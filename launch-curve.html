The Launch Curve

A hot figure sells out. The aftermarket spikes. Then it cools — fast. Here is the pattern, drawn from real reported eBay solds of newly launched figures, point by point. No index, no predictions, no invented numbers: just the timeline of what collectors actually paid, and the playbook it supports.

Archive № launch-curves

The pattern

Sellout → spike → cool → retail

Every documented launch on this page follows the same arc. Pre-orders open and the popular figures sell out within hours (GamesRadar documented this for Doomsday Wave 1's Doom, Cyclops and Gambit in August 2026). Within weeks, eBay solds peak at 2–3× MSRP — early buyers paying for immediacy. Then supply catches up, restocks land, and prices collapse back toward retail inside about six weeks. The premium is time, not rarity — and time is the one thing every collector can wait out.

When a reissue is announced the curve compresses further: speculative listings undercut each other within days. The full mechanism is on the market briefing and in the rarity guide's reissue-radar notes.

Launch playbook

What the data supports

  1. Pre-order at 1 PM ET on day one. The curve only exists because wave-1 figures sold out within hours. Grail Quest's launch-day prep builds you a checklist for every upcoming window.
  2. Never pay the launch premium. Gambit peaked at $79.99 on Sep 24, 2026 and presold at $25.99 fifteen days later — below MSRP. The spike is a tax on impatience, not a valuation.
  3. Set your watchlist target at or below MSRP for mainline waves. The Price Watch compares your target against reported ranges — and for mainline figures, retail is the ceiling history supports.
  4. Distinguish sellouts from scarcity. A "Sold Out" flag on a Pulse product page (Cyclops, Oct 2026) is a stock signal, not a grail signal. Real scarcity has a production story — see the rarity guide.
  5. Watch the reissue radar, not the hype. VHS → Retro, Retro Venom, Super-Skrull: every availability-based premium in 2026 met the same fate. Availability premiums cool; rarity premiums don't.
Methodology

Honest gaps

Curves appear only where a hobby tracker publishes at least three dated sold points for a figure — see the documented curves below. Presale points are labeled as presale and never mixed with solds. Points are reported eBay sales from the named tracker, not scraped live prices; the Chronicle publishes no price index. Nothing here is investment advice.
